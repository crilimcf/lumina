# Lumina Terms of Use

**Version:** 9 August 2026

> This text is a technical product baseline and should be reviewed by legal counsel before a broad public launch.

## 1. What Lumina is

Lumina is a social network that lets you create a profile, follow people, publish to the Feed, share temporary Moments, participate in Rooms, and communicate by message or call.

The main navigation is Feed, Rooms, New, Radar and Messages. Alerts and Profile are personal areas.

## 2. Who can use it

Lumina’s current policy requires users to be at least **16 years old** to create an account. Date of birth is requested during registration to enforce this rule.

Do not provide a false age or create an account in another person’s name without authorization.

## 3. Your account

You are responsible for keeping your password and authentication methods secure. You can enable two-step verification and view or close active sessions.

Do not share recovery codes, tokens or credentials with third parties.

## 4. Content and social features

You remain responsible for the content you publish.

- The **Feed** shows your posts and posts from people you follow in chronological order.
- **Rooms** are public or private topic spaces. Private Rooms are invitation-only.
- **Moments** remain available for 24 hours unless you delete them sooner.
- **Radar** keeps discovery and campaigns separate from the social Feed.
- **Messages** support private conversations and calls.

Private profiles require a request to be accepted before a follow relationship exists. Blocking removes the relationship and visibility between the two accounts.

## 5. What is not allowed

Do not use Lumina for:

- illegal content;
- threats, stalking or harassment;
- sexual exploitation of minors;
- unauthorized disclosure of other people’s personal data;
- spam, fraud, malware or unauthorized-access attempts;
- bypassing blocks, access controls or security mechanisms;
- deceptively impersonating another person.

## 6. Moderation

People can report accounts, posts and comments. Content that receives enough reports may be hidden automatically until it is reviewed.

Global moderation decisions are reserved for the Lumina team. We may restore or remove content and suspend accounts when necessary to enforce these Terms or comply with the law.

## 7. Media, storage and ephemeral content

Photos and videos may be stored with an external object-storage provider. The application validates type and size and associates each upload with the account that initiated it.

Messages and Moments marked as ephemeral are removed from active content according to the rules shown in the product. Lumina does not promise to prevent screenshots, recordings or copies made by another person before expiry.

## 10. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account. The product applies a 30-day window during which the request can be cancelled by signing back in. After execution, covered data is removed according to the implementation and applicable legal obligations.
